Privacy policy

Draft for the test version of the app. Last updated November 21, 2026.

Via Ardua is a gym finder for people who travel for work. This page says what the app collects and what it does not.

The short version

  • You can browse gyms without an account.
  • Your location stays on your phone. It is never sent to us.
  • No ads, no trackers, and we do not sell or share your information.

What we store if you make an account

  • Email and password. Used to sign you in. The password is stored scrambled; we cannot read it.
  • Name and trade. Shown next to your reviews. Trade is optional.
  • What you post. Ratings, reviews, photos, suggested edits and reports.
  • Saved gyms. So your list follows you to a new phone. Without an account, saved gyms stay on your phone only.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location while it is open to show gyms near you and work out distances. That happens on your phone. If you say no, the app still works: search by city instead.

Photos

The app only sees the photos you pick to upload. Photos you post are public in the app once approved.

Other services the app talks to

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and photos.
  • Apple Maps (in the app) and OpenStreetMap (on the web) draw the map. They receive the area of the map you are looking at.
  • YouTube serves video thumbnails on the Via Ardua tab. Videos open in YouTube.

Deleting your information

You can ask us to delete your account and everything you posted. Message Via Ardua and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

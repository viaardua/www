Gyms for the hard way · Wisconsin

Chalk & Iron treats the app like the basement gym Paul and his crew go looking for: cast-iron black, chalk-white type, stamped steel, and nothing shiny. Ratings are anvils, because a good gym is where you get hammered into shape.

Rating system

Rated in anvils

Every score is 1 to 5 anvils, in half steps. A filled anvil is chalk white. An empty one is cold iron. Half anvils are cut at the waist, so a 4.5 reads at a glance on the map card.

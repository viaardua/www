Gyms for the hard way · Wisconsin

Chalk & Iron treats the app like the basement gym Paul and his crew go looking for: cast-iron black, chalk-white type, stamped steel, and nothing shiny. Three working colors cut through the black: forge orange for anything you tap to act, tempered blue for facts you can trust, and warm amber for the people side of a gym. Ratings are anvils, because a good gym is where you get hammered into shape.

Your three questions

Color, a name, and a social score

Paul, you liked the format and asked three things. Here is where each one landed. Everything below is the same Chalk & Iron look, now with working color and a modern app layout.

Forge orange is for anything you tap to act. Tempered blue marks facts you can trust, like hours and your picks. Amber is only for the social side. Green means open now. One color, one meaning, everywhere.

Rating system

Rated in anvils

Every score is 1 to 5 anvils, in half steps. A filled anvil is chalk white. An empty one is cold iron. Half anvils are cut at the waist, so a 4.5 reads at a glance on the map card.

Building blocks

Colors, type and parts

Color with a job

The black and chalk stay calm and serious, so the few colors that do appear carry meaning. Each one is matched to what color psychology says it does well, and is used only for that job. That keeps the signal strong: if it's orange, you can tap it.

Forge orangeEnergy and urgency. Red-orange raises arousal and draws the eye first, so it marks every call to action: Rate this gym, Paul's pins, the active tab, filter counts.
#FF6A2B
Tempered blueTrust and reliability. Blue reads as dependable, so it marks verified facts: Paul's pick, hours, phone, directions, website.
#4FA3E0
AmberWarmth and friendliness. Warm yellow-gold reads as social and welcoming, so it belongs to the Social environment score and crowd tags.
#F2B84B
Open greenGo. Kept strictly for "open now," the one status people check before they drive over.
#8FBF7A

Color psychology gives tendencies, not guarantees. The bigger win is consistency: one color, one meaning, everywhere in the app.
